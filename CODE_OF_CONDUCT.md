# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in HORUS OS a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
level of experience, nationality, religion, or technology choices. Beginners
are especially welcome.

## Our standards
**Encouraged:** kindness, patience with newcomers, constructive feedback,
crediting others, and assuming good intent.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, publishing others'
private information, or sustained disruption.

## Scope
Applies in all project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, discussions) and in public
spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement
Report concerns to the maintainer at **alaa00saber@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or ban contributors who
violate this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
